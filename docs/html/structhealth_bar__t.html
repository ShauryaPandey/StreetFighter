Detailed Description

Health bar is not actually in charge of maintaining the health of the characters, It is only responsible for representing the health as a bar on the screen. Health bar is responsible for showing a health out of 100 in percentage or ratio. So in our case we use 2 health bars each is updated by the characters code.
